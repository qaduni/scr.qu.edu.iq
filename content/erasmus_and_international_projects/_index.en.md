# Erasmus+ & International Projects - University of Al-Qadisiyah

The University of Al-Qadisiyah is dedicated to enhancing international academic partnerships and advancing its institutional, educational, and research capacities through active participation in globally funded projects and programs.

---

## 1. Erasmus+ Projects

### 1. EDUU Project
* **Program Type:** Capacity Building in Higher Education (CBHE) – European Union
* **Role:** Beneficiary Partner
* **Objectives & Outcomes:** Institutional and academic capacity building, modernizing higher education practices, and enhancing research capabilities.

### 2. WALADU Project
* **Program Type:** Capacity Building in Higher Education (CBHE) – European Union
* **Role:** Beneficiary Partner
* **Objectives & Outcomes:** Aligning higher education learning outcomes with labor market needs and fostering student skill development.

### 3. BANUU Project
* **Program Type:** Capacity Building in Higher Education (CBHE) – European Union
* **Role:** Beneficiary Partner
* **Objectives & Outcomes:** Promoting innovation, supporting student entrepreneurship, and developing institutional governance.

### 4. ICM with Freie Universität Berlin
* **Program Type:** International Credit Mobility (ICM) – European Union
* **Role:** Academic Partner
* **Objectives & Outcomes:** Facilitating academic mobility for students and staff, promoting educational exchange, and joint research cooperation.

---

## 2. Other International Projects

### 5. Climate Literacy Project for Iraqi Higher Education
* **Funding Body:** British Council – Going Global Partnerships
* **Role:** Project Partner (in collaboration with University of Kufa and Al-Turath University)
* **Objectives & Outcomes:** Building trainer capacity, raising environmental and climate awareness, and embedding climate literacy into higher education curricula.
